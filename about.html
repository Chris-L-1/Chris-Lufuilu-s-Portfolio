My Story

Designing with purpose.

Hi, I'm Chris, a UI/UX designer based in South Africa with a genuine interest in understanding people. My design journey started at Rosebank College where I studied Web and App Development and got my first introduction to digital design tools.

Since then I have taken ownership of my own growth through self-directed study in UX and UI design. I have been building a real foundation in research, wireframing, and usability testing by working on real problems and documenting the full process from start to finish.

My work lives at the intersection of research and creativity. I use behavioral observation, empathy mapping, and information architecture analysis to understand what is actually broken before I design anything. Then I translate those findings into wireframes and solutions that speak directly to what users actually need.

When I am not designing I am learning new tools, reading about psychology and design thinking, and staying curious about how people experience technology in South Africa and beyond.

My Design Philosophy

"Design is not decoration. It is communication. Every visual decision is a choice about what matters."

I approach every project through a human centred lens. That means no assumptions, only evidence gathered from the people who will actually use what I build. I believe empathy is a design skill and that truly inclusive design requires constantly questioning your own assumptions.

My approach is grounded in three things: clarity over cleverness, consistency over novelty, and context above everything else. A solution that works well in one context can completely fail in another. That is especially true here in South Africa where digital access, literacy, and expectations vary a lot depending on who you are designing for.
