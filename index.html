About Me

Turning complexity into
clarity.

I'm a self-directed UI/UX designer who is passionate about building digital experiences that actually work for real people. My process always starts with understanding the person before touching any design tool.

I completed a three year IT programme at the College of Cape Town and have since been building my UX skills independently, working through real case studies and documenting my full process from research to wireframes to usability testing.
